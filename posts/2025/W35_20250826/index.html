W35 Tuesday

Aug 26, 2025

1 apathy

[ ˈapəthē ]
(n.) lack of feeling or emotion, lack of interest or concern.

Drug abuse leading to apathy and depression.

2 leniency

[ ˈlēnēən(t)sē ]
(n.) the quality or state of being lenient

lenient
(adj.) of mild and tolerant disposition or effect, not harsh, severe, or strict

The defense requested leniency in light of their client's lack of a prior criminal record.

3 solivagant

[ sōˈlivəgənt ]
(n.) a solitary wanderer
(adj.) rambling alone, marked by solitary wandering
独居者

4 solitary

[ ˈsäləˌterē ]
(adj.) being, living, or going alone or without companions
(n.) one who lives or seeks to live a solitary life

He took a solitary walk on the beach.
The prisoner was kept in solitary.

5 glitch

[ ˈglich ]
(n.) a usually minor malfunction, a false or spurious electronic signal

Glitches in the speaker's schedule caused some delays.

6 tempestuous

[ temˈpeschəwəs ]
(adj.) of, relating to, or resembling a tempest

Order was restored to the court after the judge put a stop to the defendant's tempestuous outburst.

7 tempest

[ ˈtempəst ]
(n.) a violent storm
(v.) to raise a tempest in or around

The sudden summertime tempest drove us off the golf course and into the clubhouse.

8 flagrant

[ ˈflāgrənt ]
(adj.) conspicuously offensive, something may be described as flagrant if it is conspicuously bad—that is, too bad to be ignored.

Her flagrant disregard for other people's rights.

9 cloister

[ ˈklȯistər ]
(n.) a monastic establishment, an area within a monastery or convent to which the religious are normally restricted
修道院，回廊

(v.) to seclude from the world in or as if in a cloister

monks living in a cloister in the country

10 undulant

[ ˈənjələnt ]
(adj.) rising and falling in waves, having a wavy form, outline, or surface

It is suspended, high in the air, above a line of low, undulant blue hills.
