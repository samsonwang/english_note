W35 Monday

Aug 25, 2025

1 mythology

[ miˈthäləjē ]
(n.) the body of customs, beliefs, stories, and sayings associated with a people, thing, or place

Ares is the god of war in Greek mythology.

2 impurity

[ imˈpyu̇rətē ]
(n.) something that is or that makes impure

impurities in the water made it cloudy

3 paratrooper

[ ˈperəˌtrüpər ]
(n.) troops trained and equipped to parachute from an airplane

paratroops

4 parachute

[ ˈperəˌshüt ]
(v.) a device for slowing the descent of a person or object through the air that consists of a fabric canopy beneath which the person or object is suspended

The pilot was wearing a parachute.

5 crop

[ ˈkräp ]
(n.) a plant or animal or plant or animal product that can be grown and harvested extensively for profit or subsistence
(v.) to remove the upper of outer parts of

Tobacco is their main crop.
Her hair was cropped short.

6 closure

[ ˈklōzhər ]
(n.) an act of closing : the condition of being closed
an often comforting or satisfying sense of finality

I need to talk to him and get some closure on this issue.

7 canopy

[ ˈkanəpē ]
(n.) a cloth covering suspended over a bed, an ornamental rooflike structure

A canopy hung over the altar.
