W34 Wednesday

Aug 20, 2025

1 euphemism

[ ˈyüfəˌmizəm ]
(n.) the substitution of an agreeable or inoffensive expression for one that may offend or suggest something unpleasant.

Cougar is the euphemism for a woman who has reached mid-life, who is single, financially secure and on the lookout for relationships with younger men—as in "prey."

cougar 美洲狮

2 intriguing

[ / inˈtrēgiŋ/ ]
(adj.) engaging the interest to a marked degree.

Nationally, the story will be mostly about Sanders, an intriguing figure once thought to be a sure first-round pick after starring for his father’s team at Colorado.

3 perish

[ ˈper-ish ]
(v.) to become destroyed or ruined

Two people perished in the fire.

4 admiralty

[ ˈadm(ə)rəltē ]
(n.) the executive department or officers formerly having general authority over British naval affairs
海军部

What was once confined to admiralty and customs skyrocketed when it was expanded to everyday use in the 1980s during the War on Drugs.

5 erebus

[ ˈerəbəs ]
(n.) a personification of darkness in Greek mythology
a place of darkness in the underworld on the way to Hades

6 inuit

[ ˈin(y)üwət ]
(n.) a group of Indigenous peoples of northern Alaska, arctic Canada, and Greenland —used especially for those of the Canadian Arctic and Greenland

In fact, many traditional northern hunter–gatherers such as the Inuit subsisted mostly on animal foods.

7 forensic

[ fəˈren(t)sik ]
(adj.) belonging to, used in, or suitable to courts of judicature or to public discussion and debate.

Court records show that forensic testing did not find evidence that the woman had been drugged.

8 anthropology

[ ˌan(t)thrəˈpäləjē ]
(n.) the science of human beings
theology dealing with the origin, nature, and destiny of human beings

9 scurvy

[ ˈskərvē ]
(n.) a disease caused by a lack of vitamin C and characterized by spongy gums, loosening of the teeth, and a bleeding into the skin and mucous membranes
坏血症

10 surgeon

[ ˈsərjən ]
(n.) a medical specialist who practices surgery : a physician trained and qualified to perform surgical procedures
